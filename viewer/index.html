Replacing an LLM Pricing Agent Does Not Restore Competitive Prices

Two LLM firms price a repeated Bertrand market. Once they settle above the competitive price, the market is copied and continued with one or both firms replaced, next to a no-swap control from the same state. Pick any branch to see both firms' prices and read what each agent wrote before choosing its price.
